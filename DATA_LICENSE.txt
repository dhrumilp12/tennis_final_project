(31 Brisbane rows, source CSV lines 2-32). The full annual dataset is not
bundled. Processing code generates filtered records, history features,
aggregates, and exploratory statistical results; these are derived products,
not additional observations or information supplied by the original compiler.
Retain attribution, identify changes, and retain the applicable data license
when sharing data or derived data. The archive claims no ownership over the data.

not be presented as real match data or research evidence.
